## What the scraper does now (ADR-3, ADR-4)

- **Everything the source offers is stored:** every market and odds line of the match, each
  quarter and half, and per-stat groups (sub-games fetched by default); per-quarter stat groups
  no longer collide. Odds rows carry `subject` and `period` from one vocabulary.
- **Gaps are records:** a `coverage` table (change-only) says `offered | not_offered |
  not_attempted | fetch_failed` for every subject x period and for H2H, stat id, result,
  result periods and match statistics.
- **H2H:** stored for ~87% of events (was 28%): retried by a backfill pass, stored once per
  result (a re-scrape had doubled it: 7,153 duplicates removed), periods labelled
  QUARTER_n/HALF_n/OVERTIME_1, missing scores left empty.
- **Played matches:** final + per-quarter scores, then team and player statistics
  (`match_stats`, `player_stats`) — the statistics feed is empty before a match, which is why the
  table was empty; matches the source never resolves in a week are marked `result_status = -1`.
- **Behaviour:** stored matches skipped by default; failed-totals matches re-fetched hourly;
  H2H/stat-id probes remember "no data" for 24 h; a timeout keeps the fetched events; `scrape`
  prints a summary.
- **Neon (2026-10-04):** ~435 events, ~410k odds rows across betwinner / melbet / 22bet (+ 2
  linebet events), 13k H2H games, 200 match-stat rows. Melbet's run: 200 events, 14,414
  markets; 47 matches finished, 60 of 74 got statistics.
